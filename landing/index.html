Who it’s for

Three roles, one rule: the list’s policy decides.

  • Submitters add entries that follow a list’s rules and get their deposit back.

    How disputes are settled

    Anyone can check an entry against its list’s rules. A challenge goes to Kleros jurors.

    1. 01

      Submit

      Add an entry with a deposit you get back.

      Submitted
    2. 02

      Open for verification

      During the challenge period, anyone can check it against the rules.

      Open to challenge
    3. No challenge

      Listed

      An entry that follows the rules is listed, and its deposit comes back.

      Registered
    4. 03 · Challenged

      Kleros Court

      The challenger adds a deposit; jurors rule on the list’s rules. Rulings can be appealed.

      Jurors deciding
    5. 04

      Ruling

      Winners get their deposit back plus the other side’s, less the court fee. For a challenger, that extra is the bounty.

      Registered Rejected

    Stake Curate keeps the stake with the entry, which anyone can challenge at any time: a successful challenger earns the whole stake, and a failed challenge adds to it. The submitter gets the stake back by withdrawing it, after a delay. Appeals and fees in the docs
